### Deskripsi lengkap
ENTEGO adalah platform ekosistem event untuk membantu kamu menemukan kebutuhan acara dalam satu tempat.

Jelajahi talent, production, photo & creative, beauty & styling, food & hospitality, organizer, venue, rental & transport, serta decoration & event support.

Dengan ENTEGO kamu dapat:
- menemukan partner berdasarkan kategori dan layanan;
- melihat profil, layanan, harga, dan portofolio partner;
- membuat dan memantau booking;
- berkomunikasi terkait order;
- melihat status transaksi, refund, dispute, dan penyelesaian layanan yang tersedia;
- mengakses kontrol privasi, support, serta jalur penutupan akun.

Untuk Mitra ENTEGO, aplikasi menyediakan fondasi pengelolaan profil bisnis, banyak layanan dalam satu akun, menu layanan dan harga, order, portofolio, serta verifikasi identitas untuk fungsi yang memerlukannya.

ENTEGO dibangun untuk layanan event dunia nyata. Fitur dan ketersediaan partner dapat berbeda menurut wilayah dan status operasional.

## English

### App name
ENTEGO

### Short description
Discover and book event services from professional partners in one ecosystem.

### Full description
ENTEGO is an event ecosystem platform designed to help customers discover event services in one place.

Explore talent, production, photo & creative, beauty & styling, food & hospitality, organizers, venues, rental & transport, and decoration & event support.

With ENTEGO you can:
- discover partners by category and service;
- view partner profiles, services, pricing and portfolios;
- create and track bookings;
- communicate about active orders;
- view available transaction, refund, dispute and service-completion status;
- access privacy controls, support and the account-closure pathway.

For ENTEGO Partners, the platform provides foundations for managing a business profile, multiple services under one account, service menus and pricing, orders, portfolios and identity verification where required.

ENTEGO is built for real-world event services. Feature and partner availability may vary by region and operational status.
