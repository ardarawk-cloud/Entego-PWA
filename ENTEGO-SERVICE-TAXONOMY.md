## Core Rule
**Category is a customer discovery tool, not an account boundary.**

One ENTEGO Professional Partner has one business identity/profile and may offer many relevant services.

Model:
`1 Partner Account → 1 Business Profile → Primary Service → Many Services Offered → Many Service Menu Items / Prices`

A partner must not create duplicate accounts merely because it offers multiple services.

Example:
**Mr Brown Sound System**
- Primary service: Sound System
- Also provides: Lighting, DJ Equipment / CDJ, LED Screen, Stage, Genset
- The same partner may appear in customer discovery for each service.
- In an unfiltered/all-partner surface, the business should be deduplicated and shown once with its service summary.

## Customer Filter Behavior
- `Semua` = show all service types.
- Selecting a main category = show only service types belonging to that category.
- Selecting a service type = discover partners whose primary service OR additional services include that service.
- One partner may match many services but remains one business identity.

## Partner Onboarding Behavior
Partner onboarding should remain simple:
1. choose primary service
2. tick all other services genuinely provided
3. build Menu Layanan & Harga
4. maintain one business profile

## Visual Rule
The category layer is a primary face of the ENTEGO APK.
- category icons must be relevant to their category
- use a consistent premium icon family
- monochrome black/dark icons are the default
- avoid generic repeated icons for unrelated categories
- prioritize clarity, polish, and fast recognition over decorative complexity

Only Arda may revise this taxonomy lock.
