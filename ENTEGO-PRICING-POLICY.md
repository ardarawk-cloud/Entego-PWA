# ENTEGO PRICING POLICY v1.0 — PARTNER SERVICE FLOOR

Owner / Final Authority: Arda
Status: OFFICIAL FOUNDATION / LOCKED
Startup command: `KAI ENTEGO START`

## Core Rule
ENTEGO uses a platform minimum bookable service price of **Rp1.000.000**.

A Professional Partner may set different prices for different services or event contexts, but an active customer-facing service price may not be below Rp1.000.000.

Examples for a DJ partner:
- Wedding After Party — Rp3.000.000
- Wedding Full Ceremony — Rp6.000.000
- Club — Rp2.000.000
- Beach Club — Rp1.500.000

These are examples only, not default prices.

## Enforcement
- `0` is allowed only as an unset/not-yet-priced state.
- Any positive customer-facing partner starting price below Rp1.000.000 must be rejected.
- Any service-menu/package price below Rp1.000.000 must be rejected.
- Enforcement must exist in both client UX and server/API validation; client-only warnings are insufficient.
- Public customer surfaces must not deliberately advertise active ENTEGO partner services below the platform floor.

## Partner Freedom Above Floor
Partners remain free to create multiple service-menu entries and set their own prices at or above the ENTEGO floor.

Each menu entry can have its own:
- service/package name
- duration or scope
- description/inclusions
- customer price
- primary/featured status

## Scope
Current v1.0 rule applies uniformly to ENTEGO Professional Partners while the marketplace is in Phase 1.

Future category-specific floors may be introduced only by explicit Arda decision. A future pricing engine may support different minimums by category, city/market, service type, duration, or event class, but no such matrix is locked yet.

## Purpose
The product intent is to prevent destructive underpricing inside ENTEGO, maintain a professional marketplace positioning, and keep partner economics sustainable.

## Governance / Legal Review Gate
This is an internal product/commercial policy. Before broad public-scale enforcement or contractual partner rollout, ENTEGO must review the final wording, partner terms, market impact, and implementation with appropriate Indonesian competition-law/legal guidance. Do not characterize the policy as an agreement among competing partners to coordinate prices.

## Authority
Only Arda may revise, suspend, or cancel the ENTEGO minimum pricing floor.
