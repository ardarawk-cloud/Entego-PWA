## Marketing Products
ENTEGO Marketing is designed as three separate products:

1. **ENTEGO Boost**
   - paid visibility / sponsored placement for a selected partner service
   - target by relevant service and area
   - sponsored results must be clearly labelled `Sponsored`
   - Boost buys visibility, not trust or reputation

2. **ENTEGO Promo**
   - partner-funded promotional price or discount on a specific service-menu item
   - final customer-facing service price must still respect ENTEGO's active minimum booking value
   - current floor: Rp1.000.000

3. **ENTEGO Campaign**
   - ENTEGO-created themed campaigns such as Wedding Month, Bali Party Week, Corporate Event Deals, or New Partner Spotlight
   - partner participation may be application/eligibility based

## Trust Firewall
The following must NEVER be sold as advertising products:
- Verified badge
- KYC approval
- customer rating
- reviews
- organic quality/reputation score

Paid visibility and organic marketplace trust must remain distinguishable.

## Discovery Rule
Sponsored placement must remain relevant to the customer's current intent.
Example: a partner boosting MUA should appear as Sponsored within relevant MUA/Beauty discovery, not above unrelated Sound System results merely because it paid more.

## Rollout
Phase 1 implementation may expose configuration/draft UX before billing is activated.
Do not represent a Boost as paid/active until ENTEGO billing and campaign activation are genuinely implemented.

## Future Partner Analytics
Target reporting model:
Impressions → Profile Views → Booking Intent → Booking → Transaction Value.
